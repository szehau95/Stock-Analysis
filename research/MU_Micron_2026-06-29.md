## PART A — FUNDAMENTAL RESEARCH BRIEF

### INVESTMENT THESIS

- **HBM is sold out through CY2026 and is structurally pre-sold, not spot-cyclical.** Micron reported an HBM (high-bandwidth memory — the DRAM stacks that sit on AI accelerators) annualized revenue run-rate of ~$8B with CY2026 capacity fully committed, and disclosed 16 strategic customer agreements (press coverage puts the aggregate value of customer commitments at ~$100B). This converts a historically commodity business into one with backlog visibility — the core of the bull case.
- **The P&L has re-rated violently and the market is paying <13x forward for it.** Fiscal Q3 2026 (quarter ended 2026-05-28) revenue was $41.46B, up from $23.86B sequentially and $9.30B a year ago (+346% YoY), at an 84.6% GAAP gross margin and 80.4% operating margin — margins unprecedented for a memory maker. Yet the stock trades at roughly 9–13x forward earnings (see Valuation), a multiple that prices in an imminent cycle roll-over.
- **The balance sheet has flipped to a fortress.** Micron ended Q3 with $30.2B cash/investments against $5.7B debt — a $24.4B net cash position — after retiring $4.4B of debt in the quarter (including a $4.3B senior-notes tender). The company funds a ~$27B FY26 capex plan internally and still guides Q4 free cash flow above $30B.

### BUSINESS MODEL

Micron designs and manufactures memory and storage: **DRAM** (~majority of revenue, including the high-margin **HBM** used in AI/data-center accelerators), **NAND** flash (SSDs and storage), and emerging high-capacity modules. It is one of three scaled DRAM producers globally and one of the top NAND suppliers.

The **moat is narrow but real and currently widening**. Memory is fundamentally a commodity — pricing is set by industry supply/demand, and Micron is a price-taker in mainstream DRAM/NAND. What is *defensible* is (1) the capital and process-technology barrier to leading-edge DRAM (only three firms can do it at scale), and (2) qualification lock-in on HBM, where customers (notably AI-accelerator vendors) design Micron silicon into multi-year platforms, creating switching friction the commodity DRAM business never had.

**Competitive position.** In the broad DRAM market, Samsung (~38%) and SK Hynix (~29%) lead, with Micron the clear #3. In **HBM specifically** — the segment that matters for the AI thesis — Q2 2026 share was **SK Hynix ~62%, Micron ~21%, Samsung ~17%**, i.e. Micron has overtaken Samsung for the #2 HBM position. On NVIDIA's HBM4 allocation specifically, reporting indicates SK Hynix mid-50%, Samsung mid-20%, Micron ~20%. Micron is shipping HBM4 samples rated up to 11 Gbps and working with foundry partners on HBM4E. The NAND peers are SanDisk (SNDK, spun out of Western Digital in 2025) and Western Digital (WDC).

### FINANCIAL SNAPSHOT *(fiscal Q3 2026, quarter ended 2026-05-28, from the Q3 8-K/press release)*

- **Revenue:** $41.46B — **+74% sequential** ($23.86B in Q2), **+346% YoY** ($9.30B a year ago).
- **Margins:** GAAP gross margin **84.6%**, operating margin **80.4%**. (Memory historically runs 20–50% gross at cycle peaks; these levels are without modern precedent and are the single figure most likely to normalize.)
- **Earnings:** GAAP net income **$28.24B** ($24.67/diluted share); non-GAAP net income **$28.86B** ($25.11/share). Both beat consensus (~$20.49 non-GAAP EPS) by >20%.
- **Cash flow:** Operating cash flow **$25.39B**; net capex **$7.08B**; **adjusted free cash flow $18.30B**.
- **Balance sheet / debt:** $30.2B cash & investments, **$5.7B total debt → $24.4B net cash**; $4.4B debt retired in-quarter.
- **ROIC / ROE:** *data unavailable* as a company-reported figure for Q3; on an annualized-run-rate basis returns are extraordinary given the net-cash, high-margin profile, but a precise primary-sourced number is not published — flagged rather than estimated.

### VALUATION

At ~$1,055 (intraday 2026-06-29) against 1,129.4M shares, market cap is ~**$1.27T** (as of the 2026-06-28 close); enterprise value is ~$1.25T after netting the $24.4B cash pile.

- **Forward P/E:** The Street quotes ~**13x** forward; on the company's *own* guidance — Q3 $25.11 + Q4 guide $31.00, i.e. a >$112 annualized run-rate — the stock is closer to **~9x**. Either way it is **below the US semiconductor industry average (~21x)** and below its peer SanDisk only on a growth-adjusted basis.
- **EV/EBITDA (enterprise value relative to operating earnings):** *data unavailable* as a clean primary figure; given ~80% operating margins on a >$160B revenue run-rate, the implied multiple is low-double-digit — directionally cheap, but flagged as not primary-sourced.
- **P/S:** ~6–7x on the annualized revenue run-rate; **P/B 16.8x** (rich on book, which lags the earnings explosion).
- **Versus peers:** SanDisk (SNDK) ~11.7x forward P/E; Western Digital (WDC) ~42.5x trailing P/E. Micron sits between them on forward earnings despite the strongest near-term growth and margin profile of the three.
- **Versus own history:** Micron has historically bottomed near book value in downturns and rarely sustained >15x forward through-cycle. The current single-digit-to-low-teens forward multiple on record margins is the market explicitly refusing to capitalize peak earnings.

**Fair value (12-month range): ~$1,250–$1,650.** Reasoning chain: anchor on a forward annualized EPS power of ~$110–125 (Q3 actual + Q4 guide, held roughly flat), apply a **deliberately compressed 11–14x** multiple to reflect that these are likely *near-peak* memory margins, and the band lands at ~$1,250–1,650. This brackets the analyst consensus average target (~$1,557) without anchoring to it. The single largest swing factor is *how long* HBM backlog defers the next pricing down-leg.

### CATALYSTS

1. **Q4 FY2026 earnings — 2026-09-29.** Guidance bar is high: revenue **$50.0B ±$1.0B**, gross margin ~**86%**, non-GAAP EPS **$31.00 ±$1.00**, FCF >$30B. Any HBM4 ramp or pricing commentary that confirms CY2027 visibility re-rates the multiple; a guide that merely *meets* could disappoint given the +270% YTD move.
2. **FY2027 capex / capacity signaling.** Management guided FY2027 capex **above the mid-$40B range** (up from ~$27B in FY26). Read-through: more capacity = the supply discipline question for the next cycle. Watch for the formal FY27 plan on the Q4 call.
3. **HBM4 / HBM4E qualification milestones.** Micron is sampling HBM4 at up to 11 Gbps; design-win confirmation on next-gen accelerator platforms (NVIDIA/AMD) is the cleanest single-event re-rate.
4. **Incremental long-term agreements (LTAs).** The base is 16 strategic agreements / ~$100B; additions extend the pre-sold runway and erode the "it's still a commodity" discount.
5. **DRAM/NAND spot & contract pricing prints** (sector data, monthly). Continued price increases validate the supercycle; the first sequential price *decline* is the early-warning bell.

### RISKS (with explicit invalidation triggers)

- **Memory is cyclical — the whole thesis is "this time the cycle is longer," not "the cycle is gone."** Mechanism: SK Hynix and Samsung add capacity into the same demand, supply outruns AI/data-center pull, contract DRAM/HBM prices roll over, and Micron's ~85% gross margin compresses toward historical 30–40% — a direct, leveraged hit to the earnings line that the multiple is built on. *Invalidation trigger: two consecutive months of sequential DRAM/HBM contract price declines, or a Micron guide showing sequential gross-margin contraction.*
- **HBM share loss to SK Hynix/Samsung at the leading edge.** Mechanism: if Micron slips on HBM4/HBM4E yields or timing, its ~20% NVIDIA allocation erodes to the incumbents, capping the highest-margin growth vector. *Trigger: a disclosed HBM4 qualification delay, or NVIDIA-allocation commentary moving Micron below ~15%.*
- **Capex arms race compresses through-cycle FCF.** The jump to >mid-$40B FY27 capex is the company itself signaling a capacity build; if demand softens after the spend, the FCF story inverts. *Trigger: FY27 FCF guidance materially below the FY26 trajectory despite rising capex.*
- **Valuation/positioning air-pocket.** Up ~270% YTD and a 13th-largest-company market cap; the stock fell ~16% in the two sessions off the 2026-06-25 ATH on heavy volume. Mechanism: crowded momentum unwinds faster than fundamentals change. *Trigger: a close below the structural support shelf identified in Part B without a fundamental cause.*

### VERDICT — **BUY** (position direction); 12-month price target **$1,500** (range $1,250–$1,650)

Reasoning chain: pre-sold HBM through CY2026 + ~$100B of customer commitments give earnings visibility a commodity memory name has never had; record margins are funding a net-cash balance sheet and >$30B/quarter FCF; and the market is capitalizing all of it at single-digit-to-low-teens forward earnings because it assumes a near-term cycle peak. The PT applies an 11–14x multiple to ~$110–125 forward EPS power — a multiple deliberately *below* the stock's own history to respect cyclicality. This is a direction-and-sizing call; **entry timing is governed by Part B.**

## PART C — SYNTHESIS: Fundamental / Technical Alignment

The two briefs **agree on direction and differ only on timing**, which is the normal and intended state — they operate on different horizons.

- **Fundamental (12-month): BUY**, PT $1,500. HBM sold out through CY26, ~$100B customer commitments, net cash, record margins at <13x forward.
- **Technical (near-term): WAIT.** The chart is constructive (OBV rising, bullish MA stack) but the *entry* at $1,055 has no edge after a parabolic run and a sharp two-day drop.

These are **not contradictory.** The **technical call governs entry timing** — wait for the $1,020–$1,029 confluence shelf or a volume reclaim of $1,089 rather than chasing mid-range. The **fundamental call governs position direction and sizing** — the 12-month bias is long, and weakness *into the structural shelves* is an opportunity to build the position, not a reason to abandon it. The single line that reconciles both: **buy the pullback into ~$1,020, stop $945 close, targeting the ATH and beyond — and only abandon the long thesis if memory contract pricing rolls over (the fundamental invalidation), independent of the chart.**
