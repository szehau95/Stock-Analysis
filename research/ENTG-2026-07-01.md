# PART A — FUNDAMENTAL RESEARCH BRIEF

## INVESTMENT THESIS
- **Consumables-heavy purity franchise is compounding through a soft cycle.** Q1 FY2026 (reported
  Apr 30, 2026) revenue was **$811.9M, +5% YoY** and above the ~$807.8M consensus, with **adjusted
  operating margin 23.6%** and **adjusted EBITDA margin 27.8%** ($226.1M) — margin expansion in a
  still-mixed WFE environment is the core of the bull case.
- **Deleveraging is the near-term value-creation lever.** Net debt **$3.3B / 3.6x net leverage** at
  Q1'26, down from 3.8x at end-2025, with management targeting **~3.0x by year-end 2026**. Each turn
  of leverage retired transfers enterprise value to equity holders without needing multiple
  expansion.
- **Content-per-wafer growth outpaces wafer-start growth.** Entegris' materials and filtration
  content rises with each node transition (EUV, advanced packaging, gate-all-around), so revenue can
  grow faster than unit volumes even in a flat-to-soft semiconductor cycle. *(Structural claim; node
  content quantification not disclosed at wafer level — flagged.)*

## BUSINESS MODEL
Entegris sells the **materials, filtration and purity-control products consumed inside a fab** — not
the capital tools. Revenue reorganized into two segments as of FY2026 reporting:
- **Advanced Purity Solutions (APS)** — $463.6M Q1'26 sales, **29.1% adj. segment margin**:
  filtration, fluid handling, gas/liquid purity, wafer-handling/packaging. The higher-margin,
  more-recurring engine.
- **Materials Solutions (MS)** — $351.1M Q1'26 sales, **22.0% adj. segment margin**: deposition
  materials, CMP slurries/pads (the CMC acquisition), specialty gases and coatings.

**Moat.** Defensible: qualification lock-in (materials are spec'd into a customer's process and
re-qualifying is costly and slow), sub-ppb purity engineering, and unit-driven recurring demand
(consumed per wafer, not per tool). Commodity-adjacent: bulk chemicals and some hardware, where
pricing is more contested. **Peers:** the standalone comparable set is thin — **MKS Instruments
(MKSI)** on the purity/handling side, with **DuPont** and **Merck KGaA** electronic-materials units
as diversified adjacencies; CMP is effectively consolidated post the 2022 CMC deal. Named market-
share figures are **data unavailable** at the segment level from primary filings.

## FINANCIAL SNAPSHOT *(as of Q1 FY2026, reported Apr 30, 2026)*
- **Revenue:** $811.9M, **+5% YoY**; sequential figure not restated here (**data unavailable** in a
  primary form as of this writing).
- **Margins:** adj. operating **23.6%**; adj. EBITDA **27.8%**. **GAAP** operating margin is
  materially lower — **heavy CMC-acquisition amortization suppresses GAAP** (GAAP diluted EPS $0.60
  vs. adj. EPS $0.86). This is the single biggest reason trailing GAAP multiples look extreme.
- **EPS:** adj. **$0.86** (vs. ~$0.75 consensus, +15%); GAAP diluted **$0.60** (up from $0.41).
  Net income **$92.0M**, +46% YoY.
- **Free cash flow:** **$143.5M** in Q1'26 (~17.7% of sales); FY2025 FCF margin was 12.7%. Cash
  **$442.7M** at quarter-end. CapEx guided to **~$250M for 2026** (below 2025).
- **Debt:** net debt **$3.3B**, **3.6x** net leverage; **$400M** remaining variable-rate term loan
  (only floating-rate piece), reduced $50M in Q1 on top of ~$300M repaid in 2025. Target **~3.0x by
  YE2026**. Full maturity-wall detail: see the 10-Q — not reproduced here.
- **ROIC/ROE:** clean, primary-sourced figures **not verified** as of this date — **stated
  unavailable** rather than estimated. (GAAP returns are depressed by acquisition intangibles; use

## VALUATION *(multiples are source- and date-sensitive — ranges given deliberately)*
- **Trailing P/E ≈ 100–106x** (e.g., 106x on ~$1.73 GAAP TTM EPS, ~Jun 22, 2026). **This is a GAAP
  artifact of CMC amortization, not a real earnings multiple** — do not anchor to it.
- **Forward P/E:** cited between **~34x and ~49x** across aggregators (StockAnalysis ~34x; GuruFocus
  ~46–49x) depending on GAAP-vs-adjusted estimate and price date. On annualized adj. EPS (~$3.2–3.4
  run-rate off Q1 $0.86 + Q2 guide midpoint ~$0.80), the **adjusted forward multiple sits in the
  low-50s x at ~$180** — rich. **Discrepancy flagged; not averaged.**
- **EV/EBITDA ≈ 24x** (EV ~$21B cited mid-May). **P/S ≈ 6.5–8x** depending on share count/price.
- **Peer frame:** semiconductor-industry median forward P/E ~38.8x; MKSI forward P/E cited ~46–65x.
  ENTG trades at a **premium to MKSI and the group median**, justified only if margin recovery and
  deleveraging both land.
- **Market cap:** **~$27B** at $179.93 on ~152M shares *(approx.)*. Reconciliation note: the
  "$22.12B" market cap cited by StockAnalysis is dated **May 13, 2026** at a lower share price — the
  stock has re-rated up materially since; the gap is a timing artifact, not a data error.

**Fair-value range (reasoned, not single-point):** On a normalized adjusted framework — mid-20s
EV/EBITDA on a recovering-cycle EBITDA base, cross-checked against ~35–45x forward adjusted EPS — a
defensible 12-month fair-value band is **~$155–$185**, with the top of the band contingent on
(a) hitting ~3.0x leverage and (b) a WFE up-cycle inflection. **At $179.93 the stock is at the upper
half of that band** — priced for successful execution, limited margin of safety.

## CATALYSTS
1. **Q2 FY2026 earnings — July 30, 2026** (verify time-of-day vs. IR before trading it). Guide: sales
   **$815–845M**, adj. EPS **$0.76–0.84**, adj. EBITDA margin **27.0–28.0%**. The bar is the margin
   line and any WFE-recovery commentary.
2. **Deleveraging milestones** toward the ~3.0x YE2026 target — each term-loan paydown is an
   incremental equity-value transfer and a de-risking event for the multiple.
3. **WFE / advanced-node demand data points** (EUV layer counts, advanced-packaging/HBM ramp, GAA
   node transitions) that lift content-per-wafer.
4. **China / export-control policy prints** — swing factor for the demand and shipping profile
   (double-edged; see Risks).
5. **CMP/materials cross-sell execution** from the CMC integration showing up in MS segment margin.

## RISKS *(with mechanisms and invalidation triggers)*
- **Valuation compression.** At a premium to MKSI and the industry median on adjusted forwards, any
  guide-down or WFE-recovery delay de-rates the multiple fast. **Invalidation trigger:** Q2 adj.
  EBITDA margin printing **below the 27% guide floor**, or FY guidance cut.
- **Leverage / rate sensitivity.** Net debt $3.3B; if FCF slips (soft cycle + CapEx), the ~3.0x
  target slips and refinancing of the term loan gets costlier. **Trigger:** net leverage **rising
  back above ~3.8x** or FCF margin falling below ~10%.
- **China exposure via export controls.** Tightened controls could remove a demand tranche and
  compress specific product lines (gases, filtration into affected fabs). **Trigger:** management
  quantifying a China revenue headwind on the Q2 call, or a new BIS rule naming affected categories.
- **CMP competition (MS segment).** Slurry/pad pricing pressure from remaining materials competitors
  hits the 22.0% MS margin directly. **Trigger:** MS segment margin declining sequentially for two
  quarters.
- **GAAP-optics risk.** The ~100x trailing GAAP P/E is a screen-repellent; passive/retail de-rating
  risk if amortization narrative isn't managed — cosmetic, but real for flows.

## VERDICT — **HOLD** · 12-month price target **$180 (range $155–$185)**
The franchise is genuine and margins + deleveraging are moving the right way, but at $179.93 the
stock already sits in the **upper half of a defensible fair-value band** and trades at a premium to
its closest peer on adjusted forwards. The reasoning chain: normalized adjusted EBITDA × mid-20s
EV/EBITDA, cross-checked to ~35–45x forward adjusted EPS, nets ~$155–185; current price leaves little
margin of safety before the July 30 print. **Direction is constructive, but the risk/reward at spot
is balanced — accumulate on weakness toward the mid-$150s–low-$160s, not at the top of the band.**
Upgrade to Buy on a confirmed WFE up-cycle inflection **or** a pullback into that lower zone.
