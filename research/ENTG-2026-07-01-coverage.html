Section I · News Assessment
The Question

Does the HF news change anything?

Short answer: it firms the demand leg of the thesis and is a plausible partial driver of today's move — but it does not move the 12-month verdict or the fair-value band.
Tiger Brokers · 01 Jul 2026 · 10:17

TSMC, Samsung & SK Hynix scramble for electronic-grade hydrofluoric acid

Industry sources report the three leading foundry/memory makers buying up electronic-grade HF. Middle-East geopolitical tension has pushed sulfur, sulfuric acid and anhydrous HF higher since the start of the year, lifting electronic-grade HF costs; some suppliers have raised prices roughly 20–30% year-to-date.

How this actually transmits to Entegris

The instinct is to read "HF price spike" as a direct windfall. It is not — and the honest read matters more than the convenient one:

  • Entegris is a peripheral HF supplier, not a price-setter. It is named in the electronic-grade HF supplier set, but the market is led by Stella Chemifa, Morita, Solvay, Honeywell and Chinese producers. A 20–30% HF price move is not a first-order revenue line for ENTG.
  • The real signal is demand tightness at the leading edge. TSMC, Samsung and SK Hynix "scrambling" for materials is a tell that advanced-node and HBM/AI capacity is running hot. That utilisation is exactly what pulls Entegris' filtration, fluid-handling and gas/liquid-purity consumables (the Advanced Purity Solutions engine, 29.1% segment margin). Demand-positive, and it corroborates the content-per-wafer thesis.
  • Input-cost inflation is a new watch-item for the Materials Solutions segment. Higher sulfuric acid / anhydrous HF feed into ENTG's own wet chemistries. In a >70% supply-gap market Entegris should hold pricing power, but MS margin (22.0%) is the line to watch on the 30 July print.
Net Effect on the Call

No change to HOLD · $180 target · $155–185 band

This is a thematic tailwind confirmation, not a re-rating catalyst for ENTG specifically. If anything it reinforces the standing discipline — because a theme-driven, sector-wide materials-tightness pop is precisely the kind of strength the technical call says not to chase. It firms conviction on the demand side and adds one margin watch-item. Accumulate on weakness toward the mid-$150s–low-$160s; let the 30 July results confirm both the demand pull and the input-cost pass-through before paying up.

Investment Thesis

  • Margin expansion in a mixed WFE tape. Q1 FY2026 (rep. 30 Apr 2026) revenue $811.9M, +5% YoY, above consensus, with adjusted operating margin 23.6% and adjusted EBITDA margin 27.8%.
  • Deleveraging transfers value to equity. Net debt $3.3B / 3.6x, down from 3.8x at end-2025, targeting ~3.0x by YE2026 — value creation that needs no multiple expansion.
  • Content-per-wafer outgrows unit volume. Materials and filtration content rises with each node transition (EUV, advanced packaging, gate-all-around), so revenue can grow faster than wafer starts. (Structural; wafer-level content not disclosed — flagged.)

Business Model & Moat

Entegris sells the materials, filtration and purity-control products consumed inside a fab — not the capital tools. Two reporting segments as of FY2026:

SegmentQ1'26 SalesAdj. MarginWhat it is
Advanced Purity Solutions$463.6M29.1%Filtration, fluid handling, gas/liquid purity, wafer handling & packaging — the recurring, higher-margin engine.

Moat — defensible: qualification lock-in (materials are spec'd into a customer's process; re-qualifying is slow and costly), sub-ppb purity engineering, unit-driven recurring demand. Commodity-adjacent: bulk chemicals and some hardware. Peers: MKS Instruments (MKSI) on purity/handling; DuPont and Merck KGaA electronic-materials units as diversified adjacencies.
