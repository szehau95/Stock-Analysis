# PART A — FUNDAMENTAL RESEARCH BRIEF

## Investment Thesis

- **Micron is printing the strongest P&L in semiconductor history and the market is paying a discount multiple for it.** FQ3 2026 (reported June 24, 2026): revenue $41.5B, +346% YoY and +74% sequentially; non-GAAP gross margin 84.9%; EPS $25.11. The FQ4 guide is $50.0B ± $1.0B revenue, ~86.0% gross margin, $31.00 ± $1.00 EPS. Annualizing the FQ4 EPS guide gives a ~$124 run-rate — roughly **7.2x forward run-rate earnings** at $890.34.
- **The take-or-pay Strategic Customer Agreements (SCAs) structurally de-commoditize a portion of the business.** 16 signed SCAs (mostly 5-year terms, CY2026–CY2030), covering ~20% of DRAM and ~a third of NAND volume, with ~$100B of remaining performance obligations (RPO) at *minimum* volumes and *minimum* prices, plus $22B of projected customer deposits/commitments (~$18B cash, landing on the balance sheet in FQ4). Contracted floor prices are stated to support gross margins "well above our peak quarterly margins in any past cycle." This is the first time memory pricing risk has been contractually capped on a large revenue base.
- **Balance sheet and cash generation fund both the capacity race and a coming capital-return step-up.** FQ3 free cash flow was a record $18.3B (44% FCF margin) on $25.4B operating cash flow; quarter-end net cash was $24.4B ($30.2B cash/investments vs. $5.7B debt, weighted average maturity April 2035, BBB+ rated). Management intends to increase capital return from December 9, 2026 and "over time" return 100% of excess cash.

## Business Model

Micron makes money selling memory (DRAM — 76% of FQ3 revenue at $31.3B) and storage (NAND — 24% at $9.9B) into data center, mobile/client, and automotive/embedded markets. FQ3 segment split: Cloud Memory (CMBU) $13.8B at 83% gross margin, Core Data Center (CDBU) $11.5B at 87%, Mobile & Client (MCBU) $11.5B at 87%, Automotive & Embedded (AEBU) $4.6B at 79%. Data center revenue alone exceeded $25B in FQ3 (>$100B annualized run rate); data center SSD revenue exceeded $5B, doubling sequentially.

**The moat, historically thin, has three new layers:**
1. **Oligopoly + AI scarcity.** Leading-edge DRAM is a three-player market (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron). Management states DRAM/NAND demand exceeds supply, with tightness persisting "beyond calendar 2027" and no stated line of sight to supply catching up; new supply requires multi-year greenfield fabs.
2. **HBM technology position.** HBM4 12-high is ramping at twice the pace of HBM3E 12-high with >$1B of HBM4 revenue already shipped; HBM4E targets CY2027 volume. Specific HBM market-share percentages: **data unavailable** in reviewed sources (management claims share gains).
3. **Contract structure.** The SCAs (take-or-pay, price floors/ceilings on ~40% of revenue when all planned SCAs are executed) convert spot-price commodity exposure into something closer to contracted capacity — customers are explicitly paying for U.S.-based supply assurance (ID1 Idaho fab: first wafers mid-CY2027; ID2 late CY2028; New York cluster under construction).

Named competitors: Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix in DRAM/HBM (SK Hynix ADRs began trading on Nasdaq in late June 2026, giving U.S. investors a direct comparable); Samsung, SK Hynix/Solidigm, Kioxia, and SanDisk in NAND. Peer market-share data: **data unavailable** from primary sources reviewed.

## Valuation

As of July 17, 2026, at $890.34 with ~1.15B shares (company-guided count): **market cap ≈ $1.02T; enterprise value ≈ $1.00T** (net of $24.4B net cash).

- **Forward P/E (FQ4-guide annualized, ~$124 EPS run rate): ~7.2x.** (P/E = price relative to a year of earnings.)
- **EV/EBIT (FQ4-guide annualized op income ~$165B): ~6.1x.** EV/EBITDA: **data unavailable** — D&A not disclosed in the prepared remarks.
- **P/S on FY26E (~$129B): ~7.9x;** EV/S on FQ4 annualized ($200B): ~5.0x.
- **Trailing P/E:** not meaningfully computable — trailing twelve months mixes pre-supercycle quarters; the market is pricing the forward run rate.
- **Vs. own history:** Micron has historically traded between roughly 5x and 15x forward earnings across memory cycles, with the *lowest* multiples at *peak* earnings — the market's standard "peak-cycle discount." Today's ~7x sits in the lower-middle of that band despite the SCA floor structure, i.e., the market is still pricing this largely as a classic cycle peak.
- **Vs. peers:** Current Samsung / SK Hynix multiples: **data unavailable** from sources reviewed (SK Hynix's Nasdaq ADR listing is recent).

**Fair value: $950–$1,240**, framed as 8–10x the $124 forward run-rate EPS. The reasoning: a pure commodity-peak framing (5–6x) is too harsh when ~40% of revenue is targeted to carry contractual price floors/ceilings, $100B of minimum-price RPO is booked, and the balance sheet is net cash with a committed capital-return step-up; but a market multiple (well above 10x) is not yet earned while ~60% of revenue remains spot/market-priced ahead of an industry-wide capacity wave landing CY2027–2028. Below ~$800 the stock discounts meaningful FY27 earnings decline; above ~$1,250 it assumes the supercycle earnings level is the new permanent base.

## Catalysts (dated where possible)

1. **FQ4 2026 earnings — expected late September 2026** (Micron's normal cadence; exact date **unconfirmed in reviewed sources — verify on investors.micron.com before trading around it**). Bar to clear: $50B ± $1B revenue, ~86% GM, $31 ± $1 EPS, plus initial FY27 framing.
2. **December 9, 2026 — capital-return step-up.** CFO stated intent to increase capital return from this date (second anniversary of the definitive CHIPS agreements), with 100% of excess cash returned over time. A large buyback/dividend announcement is a concrete re-rating event.
3. **Additional SCA signings (ongoing, next update likely at FQ4).** Management targets "approximately half or more" of revenue under SCAs vs. 16 signed today, with "substantially higher" deposits — each signing extends the contracted-revenue story.
4. **HBM4E customer qualifications through 2H CY2026** for CY2027 volume production; qual wins/losses at the lead customer will move share expectations for both Micron and SK Hynix.
5. **Monthly DRAM/NAND contract-pricing prints (continuous).** The single most-watched external data series; the stock now trades on the second derivative of memory pricing.

## Risks — Bear Mechanisms and Invalidation Triggers

1. **The supply response is the thesis-killer.** Micron's own capex is ~$27B in FY26 rising further in FY27 (more than half of the increase construction capex); Samsung and SK Hynix are expanding in parallel. Mechanism: greenfield capacity lands CY2027–2028 → the ~60% of revenue *not* under fixed/ceiling pricing reprices down → gross margin compresses from the mid-80s toward historical-cycle levels (30–50%) → EPS run-rate halves or worse. This is exactly what the June 23 sell-off (South Korean capacity warnings, per Forbes) and the July slide were sniffing at. **Trigger: two consecutive months of falling DRAM contract prices, or a major Samsung greenfield acceleration announcement.**
2. **HBM share loss at the lead customer.** SK Hynix (the historical HBM share leader) responding on HBM4/HBM4E could claw back allocation, hitting the highest-margin line (CMBU, 83% GM). **Trigger: a competitor HBM4E qualification win announced at Micron's lead customer, or HBM ASP erosion cited in FQ1 2027 guidance.**
3. **AI capex digestion.** FQ4 guidance assumes high-teens CY2026 server unit growth. If hyperscaler capex guides flatten, the $50B guide becomes the cycle top rather than a step. **Trigger: FQ4 revenue below ~$48B or FQ1 2027 guided down sequentially.**
4. **SCA counterparty concentration.** Four "very large" customers anchor the SCAs. Take-or-pay contracts have historically been renegotiated in downturns; deposits ($18B cash) mitigate but don't eliminate this. **Trigger: any 8-K amending SCA terms or deposit schedules.**
5. **Positioning/sentiment fragility.** The stock is +212% YTD and rose ~8x in 12 months (52-week range $103.22–$1,255.00); a single Warren Buffett valuation comment knocked it down 7.7% on July 15 (per Motley Fool/Yahoo Finance). Crowded ownership means fundamental news transmits with leverage. **Trigger: technical — a daily close below the $672–$684 shelf (see Part B) would signal distribution has become a trend change.**
6. **Geopolitics explicitly excluded from guidance.** Management flags that trade/geopolitical impacts are not in the outlook; China/tariff shocks would hit numbers with no cushion.

## Verdict — BUY (12-month PT $1,100)

Reasoning chain: FQ4-guide annualized EPS ≈ $124 → apply ~9x, the mid-point of the peak-cycle discount band, justified above trough multiples by $100B minimum-price RPO, take-or-pay floors on a growing revenue share, $24.4B net cash, and the December 2026 capital-return step-up → ~$1,116, rounded to **$1,100** (+24% from $890.34). The position is a bet that contracted floors make the earnings base more durable than any prior Micron cycle — not a bet that 86% gross margins are permanent. Respect the invalidation triggers above; this is a Buy with a defined tripwire, not a conviction hold-through-anything.
