## MODULE 2 — QUALITY-OF-EARNINGS CHECK (the crux this quarter)

a) **Headline:** diluted EPS **$9.11**; net income to common **$112.1B** (8-K).

b) **The distortion, isolated:** "Other income (expense), net" was **+$97,983M**, of which **gain on equity securities = $99,031M** — overwhelmingly the mark-to-fair-value of Alphabet's ~14% Anthropic stake, whose implied valuation roughly tripled ($380B → ~$965B) on a $65B raise during the quarter. FASB ASU 2016-01 forces this unrealized markup straight through net income each quarter, **without a dollar changing hands.**

c) **Core EPS math (per the 8-K's own disclosure):** the equity-securities gain increased the tax provision, net income, and diluted EPS by **$21.9B, $77.1B, and $6.26** respectively. So:
   - Core EPS = $9.11 − $6.26 = **~$2.85**
   - vs operational consensus **~$2.87** → **essentially in-line, a hair light.**

d) **MANDATORY FLAG:** **The headline EPS beat is driven ENTIRELY by a non-operating unrealized gain; core operating EPS was roughly in-line (marginally below the ~$2.87 consensus).** The clean operating beat is on the **revenue and margin lines** (rev +2.5%, op. margin 34% vs 32%), not on EPS. Any read that treats $9.11 vs ~$8.38 as a "$0.73 operating beat" is wrong — that gap is just the Anthropic markup coming in larger than modeled.

> The tell that the market agrees: the stock fell ~4% *despite* the $99B paper gain. Investors correctly discounted the accounting profit and traded the real debate (capex/FCF). That makes the reaction higher-quality signal, not lower — see Module 5B.4.

**Subscriptions/platforms/devices — +15.2% ($12.9B).** Healthy, subscription-led (YouTube Premium/TV, One). Least-scrutinized line; quietly compounding.
